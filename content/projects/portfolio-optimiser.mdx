## The question

How far does textbook portfolio theory get you with real data?

## Data

Five years of daily prices for eight ETFs across five asset classes (UK and global equity, gilts, US Treasuries, index-linked gilts, gold, UK corporate bonds, UK property).

## Result

The maximum-Sharpe portfolio was 56% gold and 44% global equity, a Sharpe ratio of 0.76.

## What broke

The optimiser concentrated into two assets because it exploits noise in the estimated returns. Fixes would be weight caps and shrinkage estimators such as Ledoit–Wolf. The sample includes Covid, the 2022 rate rises and the gilt crisis.
