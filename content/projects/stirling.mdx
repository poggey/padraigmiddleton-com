## The idea

Replicate a junior analyst’s daily routine. Pull market and macro data, rank what mattered, and publish one permanent edition per day.

## How it works

Every evening a scheduled job fetches all sources, computes how unusual each move was, picks the Story of the Day with a transparent scoring rule, and writes an immutable edition. A second job mirrors each edition into the repo as an open dataset.

## Principle

The code decides what mattered; the AI only words it. If the AI step fails, a template takes over.

## Features worth naming

A yield curve observatory, a central bank decision countdown, a Time Machine to replay any past edition, and an interview-prep mode.

## Quality

46 fixture-backed tests, no live calls in tests.
