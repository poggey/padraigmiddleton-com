## The question

Is a film studio’s slate more like a balanced portfolio or a venture fund?

## Data

118 films tracked, a core sample of 58, collected from public APIs and cleaned by hand.

## Finding 1, returns are extremely skewed

$1.62bn deployed for a net loss of about $37m. The mean return was +191% but the median was −10%, only 43% of films were profitable, and five films made about 65% of all the profit. It behaves like a venture fund.

## Finding 2, acclaim pays

Ten Metascore points are associated with roughly a 60% higher revenue multiple (p = 0.008), and the result held across all 13 sensitivity runs.
