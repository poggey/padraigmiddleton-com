## Method

Each lap’s gap to the session best is modelled as circuit plus car-season plus driver plus noise, fitted with ridge-penalised least squares. Uncertainty from a 300-refit bootstrap, so every rating has a confidence interval.

## The insight

Driver and car can only be separated because drivers change teams. The 2021–2023 seasons alone break into disconnected islands, so the data window goes back to 2018.
