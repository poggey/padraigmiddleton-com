## Why I built it

I built this because the SpaceX listing was the biggest IPO there has ever been, and it was happening while I watched, so I could follow the fixed $135 price, the first trade and the slide afterwards as they happened rather than reading about them years later.

## My view

My own read, on the record in C7.5, is that the valuation rests largely on promises, and whether Musk keeps them is unknown given his track record. As a piece of execution the $135 was roughly right, but the aftermarket traded the ‘Musk Story’ rather than the business, and outside Starlink not much of the price is ‘real’ yet.

## What I’d do differently

If I did it again, I’d wire in live prices from the first day instead of building on stub figures shaped to news reports; when the real data arrived, the peak turned out to be an intraday print from three sessions in, not the slow climb I’d sketched. The view stays up, dated 11 July 2026, whether it ages well or not.
