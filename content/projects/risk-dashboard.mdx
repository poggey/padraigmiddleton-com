## The question

Once you’ve built a portfolio, how do you measure its risk, and how do you test it against a crisis?

## What it does

Three tabs. A risk dashboard (annualised return and volatility, Sharpe, Sortino, maximum drawdown, 95% VaR and CVaR, beta, rolling Sharpe, correlation heatmap). A “Sleep Test” that scores a portfolio against how much loss the investor says they can tolerate. A stress test that shocks factor exposures (market, rates, volatility) rather than single assets, with V, U and L-shaped crisis paths modelled on 2020, 2008 and Japan in the 1990s.

## Why factor shocks

“What if this stock falls 40%” ignores how assets move together. Shocking the underlying factors gives a more honest picture of a portfolio in a crisis.

## Limitations

Historical data only, volatility used as a proxy for duration, linear recovery paths, no correlation spikes in crises, no transaction costs.
